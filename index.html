📋 Origin Story

Who is Abhijeet.

I'm Abhijeet Sharma — a B.Tech ECE student at IIITA with a 8.13 CGPA. Like any good day in Danville, I wake up with a plan and a whiteboard full of ideas — except mine tend to involve dp problems instead of roller coasters.

I am completely obsessed with the tension between pure logic and raw creativity. On one hand, my brain is wired for the hard, uncompromising reality of engineering. I love deconstructing complex systems, getting into the weeds of the math, and figuring out the invisible architecture of how the world works.

But if I only lived in a world of variables and algorithms, I’d go out of my mind. I need the creative chaos just as much. I’m a storyteller, a community builder, and a poet; I'm a Senior Member of Sarasva (Literary Society). Even Perry would be impressed.
